Set up SSO

Flowpoint supports SAML 2.0 single sign-on for Business and Enterprise plans.

Prerequisites

  • Admin access in Flowpoint
  • An identity provider that supports SAML 2.0

Steps

  1. Open Settings → Security → SSO
  2. Upload your IdP metadata XML or enter the ACS URL and Entity ID
  3. Map the email attribute and enable Enforce SSO

After enabling, users must authenticate through your IdP. Password login is disabled for enforced domains.
